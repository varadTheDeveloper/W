# Product photo sources

Copied from varadTheDeveloper/C (branch research-data, research/photos/SOURCES.md).
The pictures `pc-*.jpg` and `setup-*.jpg` and the banner cut-outs in `public/hero/` are made
from these photos by `scripts/make-setup-photos.py` and are shown as representative images.

## Original notes

All 33 photos in `products/` are official brand photos, downloaded through the user's Chrome on
2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17, then trimmed and resized to square JPEGs on white. **None come from Amazon,
Flipkart or other shops' websites.** They are the same files the site uses in `pc-shop/assets/products/`.

`shop/` is empty: no shop, owner or signboard photos were downloaded (see "Not collected" below).

## Caveats

- Several photos show a **related model, not the exact one** sold:
  - `amd-ryzen-5.jpg` is the 5600X box, used for 5600GT / 5600 items.
  - `amd-ryzen-7.jpg` is the 5800X box, used for the 5700X item.
  - `hp-elitebook-840.jpg` is a generic EliteBook 840 shot, not specifically the G7.
  - `intel-core-i5/i7.jpg` are Intel Core badges, not boxes.
- The shop's own PC builds have no photo of the actual build; they show the processor box.

## Not collected

- No photos from the shop's Instagram (only post captions were read).
- No shop, owner or signboard photos. The Google Maps signboard photo was only viewed.
- Brand sites blocked for browser automation: lenovo.com / static.pub, asus.com (global), samsung.com,
  zebronics.com (main site). Google Images then showed a captcha, so searching was stopped there.
